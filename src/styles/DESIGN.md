## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a hyperlocal, trust-critical pet care and pet sitting ecosystem. It bridges the emotional bond between pet parents and verified caregivers. 

### Brand Personality
- **Nurturing & Reliable:** Inspires immediate confidence that beloved pets are in safe, professional, and loving hands.
- **Approachable & Warm:** Inviting, cheerful, and unpretentious, celebrating the joy and companionship pets bring to daily life.
- **Effortless & Contemporary:** Clean visual balance prioritizing smooth mobile workflows, quick discovery, and stress-free communication.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The system adopts **Warm Modernism with Soft Tactility**:
- Crisp, airy canvas spaces paired with generous border radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`).
- High-contrast readability, soft tinted neutral backgrounds, and organic ambient shadows.
- Friendly contextual iconography (paw prints, heart shields, location pins) integrated cleanly to enhance trust badges and verify credentials without clutter.
- Human-centric copy styling reflecting warm Indonesian hospitality (*"Temukan teman terbaik untuk anabul kesayangan"*).

## Colors

The palette balances clinical veterinary dependability with the playful, sunny nature of active pets.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary Teal (`#0D9488`):** Anchors trust, safety, verified credentials, primary action buttons, active navigation states, and main app headers. Evokes health and dedicated care.
- **Secondary Paw Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Represents joy, energy, and warmth. Deployed for ratings, review stars, active status accents, special promotional highlights, and key delight moments.
- **Tertiary Soft Orange (`#FB923C`):** Accent color for pet tags, pending bookings, badge backgrounds, and warm illustration accents.
- **Neutrals (`#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`):**
  - **Main Canvas Background:** `#F8FAFC` (ultra-soft cool slate that cools down device screens and reduces eye strain outdoors).
  - **Card & Surface Background:** `#FFFFFF` (crisp elevated containers for high legibility).
  - **Subtle Surface Container:** `#F1F5F9` (for secondary segments, input field fills, and list item insets).
  - **Border & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` (thin, crisp boundary definitions).
  - **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (deep slate for highest contrast readability).
  - **Text Muted / Icons:** `#64748B` (neutral, balanced clarity for metadata and placeholders).
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - **Success:** `#10B981` (Verified sitter, completed walk).
  - **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Pending review, payment due).
  - **Error:** `#EF4444` (Booking cancelled, urgent emergency note).

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the single typographic voice across all viewports. Its friendly geometry, open apertures, and subtle contemporary warmth create an inviting, readable experience on high-density mobile screens.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used sparingly for onboarding greetings (*"Halo, Paw Rent!"*) and primary discovery intros.
- **Section Headers (`headline-md` & `headline-sm`):** Anchor sitter cards, booking details, and profile tabs. Always set with tight tracking (`-0.01em`) to maintain punchy visual density.
- **Body Text:** Generous line-heights (minimum 1.5x font size) ensure effortless scanning while users are on the move with pets.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Used for badges (*"Terverifikasi"*, *"Top Sitter"*), metadata tags, prices per hour, and distance indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, targeting hybrid native containers (iOS / Android) and progressive web views.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid stack with `margin-mobile` (16px) canvas gutters. Component cards extend edge-to-edge within the margin bounds to maximize tappable surface area.
- **Tablet / Large Foldables (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Allows sitter search listings and interactive maps to render side-by-side.
- **Desktop / Web Portal (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed container (max-width 1200px centered), 24px gutters, providing clear views for pet sitter dashboards and schedule management.

### Touch Ergonomics
- All interactive elements adhere to a strict minimum touch target of **48x48px** to support single-hand mobile use.
- Critical actions (book walker, contact sitter, approve request) reside within the bottom thumb zone (0px to 140px from bottom edge), supported by fixed bottom navigation docks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Soft Organic Ambient Elevation** that feels natural and airy.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`. No shadow. Applied to main page backgrounds, inactive segment backgrounds, and recessed search bars.
- **Level 1 (Card & Card Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an ultra-soft dual shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.05);`
  Reinforced with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) to maintain separation against light backgrounds.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Popovers & Active Cards):** Used for elevated sitter profile cards during map pin selection and dropdown menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation, Modals & Floating CTAs):** Bottom sheet modals and sticky bottom booking bars:
  `box-shadow: 0 -4px 20px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);`
  Combined with subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) when overlaid on content feeds.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes friendliness, approachability, and organic warmth via high-curvature corner radiuses.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Buttons, search bars, role toggle switches, and status tags. Completely circular end caps eliminate sharp corners, communicating a playful, welcoming aesthetic.
- **Large Cards (`rounded-2xl` / 16px to 20px):** Sitter profile cards, pet bio containers, interactive map preview sheets.
- **Feature Banners & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-3xl` / 24px to 28px):** Modal top caps, promo banners, and onboarding cards.
- **Avatars & Pet Photos:** Continuous squircle or full circle shapes bordered by a 2px `#FFFFFF` halo to pop cleanly over background cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill CTA):** Height: 52px (min 48px). Background: Primary Teal (`#0D9488`), text: White, font: `label-lg`. Pressed state darkens to `#0F766E`. Includes lead icons (paw, calendar) spaced 8px from text.
- **Secondary Action:** Height: 52px. Transparent background with a 1.5px border in `#0D9488`, text: `#0D9488`.
- **Social Login Buttons:** Height: 50px. Pure white background with `#E2E8F0` border, centered brand icon (Google / Apple), text: `#0F172A`, `body-md-semibold`.

### Segmented Role Toggle (Pemilik Hewan vs. Mitra Sitter)
- A pill-shaped container (`#F1F5F9`) with 4px internal padding.
- Active state: Pure `#FFFFFF` pill with a crisp ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), text colored `#0D9488`, `label-md`.
- Inactive state: Transparent pill, text colored `#64748B`. Smooth sliding indicator transition (200ms ease-out).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Height:** 52px with soft rounded container (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`).
- **Styling:** `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E2E8F0` border. On focus: border shifts to 2px `#0D9488` with a teal glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).
- **Lead / Trail Elements:** 20px icons (map pin, search, lock, paw) tinted `#64748B` that turn `#0D9488` on field focus.

### Sitter & Pet Cards
- White surface (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-2xl`, wrapped in 1px `#E2E8F0`.
- Top row: Sitter avatar (64px circle) with verification checkmark badge, accompanied by sitter name, distance tag (*"1.2 km"*), and amber rating star pill (*"4.9 (84)"*).
- Middle row: Pet specialty chips (*"Anjing Kecil"*, *"Kucing"*, *"Dog Walking"*).
- Bottom row: Hourly rate in prominent `headline-sm` Teal (`"Rp 45.000 / jam"`) paired with a quick *"Pesan"* pill button.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Trust Badges:** Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`), vertical padding 4px, horizontal padding 10px.
- **Verified Sitter Badge:** Soft emerald background (`#CCFBF1`), dark teal text (`#115E59`), with an integrated shield-check icon.
- **Category Chips:** Light slate container (`#F1F5F9`), `#475569` text. When active: Primary Teal fill (`#0D9488`) with pure white text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- 22x22px target with `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio).
- Active: `#0D9488` fill with clean white interior icon.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
